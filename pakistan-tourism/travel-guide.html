Travel Guide

Seasons, packing, transport, etiquette and responsible travel.

Home / Travel Guide

General guidance

Always confirm current conditions, permits and advisories with official sources before travelling.

Best seasons

Summer (roughly May to September) suits the high north. Winter (roughly November to February) is pleasant in the south and Sindh. Spring and autumn are comfortable in many cities.

Packing

Layers, sturdy shoes, sun protection, a power bank, a reusable bottle, modest clothing and a basic first-aid kit.

Transport

Options include domestic flights, intercity buses and trains, ride-hailing in cities, and hired vehicles with drivers in mountain areas.

Etiquette

Dress modestly, remove shoes at mosques and homes when asked, greet politely and ask before taking photos of people.

Travel responsibly

  • Respect local rules and sacred places.
  • Support local guides, hotels and artisans.
  • Leave natural sites as you found them.
