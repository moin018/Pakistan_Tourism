Natural Wonders

Mountains, lakes, valleys, deserts, waterfalls and coast.

Home / Natural Wonders

Landscapes of Pakistan

A varied country in one border.

Water

Lakes

Saif-ul-Malook, Attabad Lake and Rama Lake offer calm water against mountain backdrops.

Peaks

Mountains

Home to the Karakoram, Himalaya and Hindu Kush, including K2 and Nanga Parbat.

Green

Valleys & Forests

Swat, Neelum, Kaghan and the Deosai Plains show Pakistan's greener side.

Desert

Deserts

Thar in Sindh and Cholistan in Punjab offer wide, quiet dunes and rich folk traditions.

Water

Waterfalls

Seasonal falls in the northern hills, best seen after the snowmelt and rains.

Coast

Coast

Beaches near Karachi and the Makran Coast, including Kund Malir and Hingol National Park.

Enjoying nature responsibly

Practical ideas to help you get more from the trip.

Leave no trace

Carry out all rubbish, including food waste.

Respect wildlife

Watch from a distance and never feed animals.

Mind the altitude

Ascend gradually and rest if you feel unwell.

Follow local guidance

Rangers and hosts know current conditions.
