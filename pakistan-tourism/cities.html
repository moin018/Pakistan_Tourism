Cities of Pakistan

Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Peshawar, Quetta and Multan.

Home / Cities of Pakistan

Urban Pakistan

Each city has its own character, food and history.

City

Lahore

The cultural capital of Punjab: Mughal monuments, the Walled City, food streets and literary life.

City

Karachi

A busy port city on the Arabian Sea with beaches, Quaid's Mausoleum and a lively food scene.

City

Islamabad

A planned capital at the foot of the Margalla Hills, home to Faisal Mosque and Daman-e-Koh.

City

Peshawar

One of South Asia's oldest living cities, with Qissa Khwani Bazaar and Bala Hisar Fort.

City

Quetta

A mountain-ringed city in Balochistan, known for fruit markets and nearby Hanna Lake.

City

Multan

The City of Saints, famous for Sufi shrines, blue tilework and handmade pottery.

Making the most of city visits

Practical ideas to help you get more from the trip.

Start early

Mornings are cooler and bazaars are quieter.

Try local food streets

Ask locals and hotel staff for trusted places.

Use ride-hailing

Handy for short trips and finding your way around.

Mind prayer times

Some mosques limit visitors at prayer times.
