Echoes of the past

From the Indus Valley to the Mughal era.

Heritage

Lahore Fort

A UNESCO World Heritage Site in the Walled City with palaces, halls and the Sheesh Mahal.

Heritage

Badshahi Mosque

A grand Mughal mosque completed in the 17th century beside Lahore Fort.

Heritage

Mohenjo-daro

Ruins of a Bronze Age Indus Valley city in Sindh, listed by UNESCO.

Heritage

Taxila

An ancient centre of Gandhara civilisation near Islamabad with monasteries and a museum.

Heritage

Makli Necropolis

One of the largest funerary sites in the world, near Thatta, with tombs spanning centuries.

Heritage

Rohtas Fort

A 16th-century fortress near Jhelum built under Sher Shah Suri, a UNESCO site.

Visiting heritage sites

Practical ideas to help you get more from the trip.

Dress modestly

Cover shoulders and knees, especially at mosques and shrines.

Go with a guide

Local guides add stories you will not find on signs.

Do not touch carvings

Oils from hands damage old stone and tile.

Check opening times

Confirm locally, as hours can change.
