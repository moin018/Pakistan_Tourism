Answers about domestic travel in Pakistan.

Home / Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Select a question to expand it.

When is the best time to visit the north?

Generally late spring to early autumn, but check road and weather conditions for your route.

What should I pack?

Layers, sturdy shoes, sun protection, a power bank and modest clothing.

How do I get around?

Flights, buses, trains, ride-hailing in cities and hired vehicles in mountain areas.

What local customs should I know?

Dress modestly, greet politely and ask before photographing people.

How can I travel responsibly?

Support local businesses, avoid littering and respect heritage sites.

Are the itineraries official packages?

No. They are suggestions only.
