Why travel Pakistan

A country of contrasts, packed into one journey.

🏔

Mountains like nowhere else

Pakistan holds the Karakoram, Himalaya and Hindu Kush ranges, with some of the highest peaks on Earth.

🕌

Layers of history

Indus Valley ruins, Gandhara monasteries, Mughal forts and Sufi shrines sit side by side.

🍛

Food worth the trip

Every region cooks differently, from Peshawari kebabs to Sindhi biryani and Balochi sajji.

🎨

Living crafts

Blue pottery, ajrak prints, embroidery and woodwork are still made by hand in many regions.

Featured destinations

Six places that show the range of the country.

North

Hunza Valley

A high valley in Gilgit-Baltistan known for apricot orchards, Baltit and Altit forts, and views of Rakaposhi and Ultar Sar.

North

Skardu

Gateway to the Karakoram, with Shangrila Lake, Shigar Fort and the cold desert of Katpana.

North

Fairy Meadows

A grassy plateau beneath Nanga Parbat, reached by a jeep track and a trek from Raikot Bridge.

City

Lahore

The cultural capital of Punjab: Mughal monuments, the Walled City, food streets and literary life.

Heritage

Lahore Fort

A UNESCO World Heritage Site in the Walled City with palaces, halls and the Sheesh Mahal.

Heritage

Badshahi Mosque

A grand Mughal mosque completed in the 17th century beside Lahore Fort.

Find your season

General guidance only: always check current road and weather conditions.

Spring

Mild weather in most cities and blossoming valleys, a lovely time for Lahore, Islamabad and Swat.

Summer

The high north opens up: Hunza, Skardu, Fairy Meadows and Deosai are at their most accessible.

Autumn

Golden light in the mountains, clear skies and comfortable days across many regions.

Winter

Pleasant in Sindh and the coast; expect snow and closures in the high mountains.

Popular experiences

Ideas for different kinds of travellers.

Trek

Trekking & Hiking

Routes include the Fairy Meadows trek and the walk toward K2 Base Camp for experienced trekkers.

Safari

Jeep Safaris

Jeep tracks reach places like Fairy Meadows' base, Deosai and the Thar desert.

Festivals

Traditions & Festivals

Eid celebrations, Basant traditions in Punjab, Shandur Polo Festival and Kalash festivals reflect regional diversity.

Stew

Nihari

A slow-cooked beef or mutton stew, often enjoyed at breakfast in Lahore and Karachi.

Water

Lakes

Saif-ul-Malook, Attabad Lake and Rama Lake offer calm water against mountain backdrops.

Crafts

Handicrafts

Multani pottery, Sindhi ajrak, Swati woodwork, Balochi embroidery and Kashmiri shawls.

A short introduction to Pakistan

Pakistan stretches from the Arabian Sea in the south to the Karakoram mountains in the north. Travellers can visit Indus Valley ruins, Mughal monuments, Sufi shrines, alpine lakes and busy bazaars, each shaped by a different regional culture.
