Pakistani Cuisine

Regional dishes and sweets worth travelling for.

Home / Pakistani Cuisine

Taste the regions

A small selection of well-known dishes.

Rice

Biryani

Fragrant spiced rice with meat, popular in Karachi and Sindh with many regional styles.

Grill

Chapli Kebab

Flat spiced minced-meat patty from Peshawar and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Roast

Sajji

Slow-roasted lamb or chicken from Balochistan, traditionally cooked over open fire.

Stew

Nihari

A slow-cooked beef or mutton stew, often enjoyed at breakfast in Lahore and Karachi.

Curry

Karahi

Meat cooked in a wok-style pan with tomatoes, ginger and green chillies.

Sweets

Traditional Sweets

Gulab jamun, jalebi, barfi, halwa puri and Multani sohan halwa.

Eating well while travelling

Practical ideas to help you get more from the trip.

Eat where it is busy

Busy places usually mean fresh food.

Ask about spice

Request mild if you are unsure.

Try breakfast classics

Halwa puri and nihari are morning favourites.

Carry clean water

Choose sealed or filtered water.
