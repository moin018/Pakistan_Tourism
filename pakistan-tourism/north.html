Northern Pakistan

Hunza, Skardu, Fairy Meadows, Naran, Kaghan and Swat.

Home / Northern Pakistan

Mountains and valleys

Road access and weather change by season, so check conditions before you travel.

North

Hunza Valley

A high valley in Gilgit-Baltistan known for apricot orchards, Baltit and Altit forts, and views of Rakaposhi and Ultar Sar.

North

Skardu

Gateway to the Karakoram, with Shangrila Lake, Shigar Fort and the cold desert of Katpana.

North

Fairy Meadows

A grassy plateau beneath Nanga Parbat, reached by a jeep track and a trek from Raikot Bridge.

North

Naran

A Kaghan Valley base town on the Kunhar River, close to Saif-ul-Malook and Lulusar lakes.

North

Kaghan Valley

Pine forests, alpine lakes and meadows running north from Balakot toward Babusar Pass.

North

Swat Valley

Green hills, rivers and Buddhist heritage in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, often called the Switzerland of the East.

Before you go

Practical ideas to help you get more from the trip.

Check the roads

Mountain roads can close with snow or landslides; confirm your route locally.

Allow buffer days

Weather and long drives can change plans, so keep spare time.

Hire local guides

Guides know trails, conditions and village customs.

Pack warm layers

Nights are cold even in summer at altitude.
