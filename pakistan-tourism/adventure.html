Adventure Tourism

Trekking, camping, climbing, jeep safaris and boating.

Home / Adventure Tourism

Get outdoors

Use licensed guides and check permits for remote or high-altitude routes.

Trek

Trekking & Hiking

Routes include the Fairy Meadows trek and the walk toward K2 Base Camp for experienced trekkers.

Camp

Camping

Campsites near Deosai, Fairy Meadows and Shangrila suit travellers who want quiet nights under stars.

Climb

Mountain Climbing

Pakistan hosts some of the world's highest peaks; serious climbs need licensed guides and permits.

Safari

Jeep Safaris

Jeep tracks reach places like Fairy Meadows' base, Deosai and the Thar desert.

Boat

Boating

Boat rides are available on lakes such as Attabad, Saif-ul-Malook and Rawal Lake.

Walk

Cultural Treks

Short village walks in Hunza, Chitral and Kalash valleys connect scenery with local life.
