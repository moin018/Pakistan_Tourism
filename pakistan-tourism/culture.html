Culture and Traditions

Festivals, clothing, crafts, music and hospitality.

Home / Culture and Traditions

Living traditions

Pakistan's regions each have distinct customs.

Festivals

Traditions & Festivals

Eid celebrations, Basant traditions in Punjab, Shandur Polo Festival and Kalash festivals reflect regional diversity.

Crafts

Handicrafts

Multani pottery, Sindhi ajrak, Swati woodwork, Balochi embroidery and Kashmiri shawls.

Dress

Clothing

Shalwar kameez is nationwide, with regional styles such as Chitrali caps, Sindhi caps and Balochi dresses.

Music

Music

Qawwali, ghazal, folk songs and regional instruments such as the rubab and dhol.

Welcome

Hospitality

Guests are often welcomed with tea and food; accepting an invitation is a warm gesture.

Voices

Languages

Urdu connects the country, alongside Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto, Balochi, Saraiki and many others.

Meeting people respectfully

Practical ideas to help you get more from the trip.

Learn a few greetings

A simple salaam goes a long way.

Ask before photographing

Especially women, children and religious ceremonies.

Accept tea graciously

Hospitality is central to many regions.

Dress for the setting

Modest clothing is appreciated in most places.
